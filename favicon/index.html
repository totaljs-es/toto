Toto's face on our websites

Official face icon: it keeps Toto's wink and smile, with both ears complete and no body or tail. The favicons have transparent backgrounds; the Apple icon uses a Paper (#FFF9F2) background and extra margin. Their use follows Toto's license.

Files

  • Kit to copy to another website: icons, original, this guide and license.
  • Original face PNG: master image with transparency.
  • favicon.ico: includes 16, 32 and 48 px.
  • PNG: 16, 32, 48, 64, 192 and 512 px.
  • apple-touch-icon.png: 180 × 180 px.

Integration

Copy the icons to assets/brand/ on the website and add these tags to its head. Adjust the paths if the application lives under a subdirectory. Toto's English, Spanish and 404 pages already include them.

<link rel="icon" href="/assets/brand/favicon.ico" sizes="16x16 32x32 48x48">
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="32x32" href="/assets/brand/favicon-32.png">
<link rel="icon" type="image/png" sizes="16x16" href="/assets/brand/favicon-16.png">
<link rel="apple-touch-icon" sizes="180x180" href="/assets/brand/apple-touch-icon.png">

As with any use of Toto, a site that shows him must state that it was built with Total.js, with a link to totaljs.com or totaljs.es. See the license.
