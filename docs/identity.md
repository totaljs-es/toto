# Toto's identity

Toto is the official mascot of **Spanish Total.js**. This guide helps create and review materials that show him. Other uses follow the [license](../LICENSE.md).

## Reference image

The [original portrait](../assets/poses/toto-signature.png) defines his main look: a softly shaded 3D illustration finish, rounded shapes, orange fur, an expressive gaze and a recognizable silhouette.

## A European red fox

Toto is a **European red fox** (*Vulpes vulpes*), not a fennec or a fantasy fox. When presenting him, always name the species and respect the traits that define it: reddish-orange fur, dark-edged ears, dark paws and a big tail with a light tip.

## Traits we keep

- Large, pointed ears with a dark edge and a light pink interior.
- A characteristic tuft over the forehead.
- Big amber and brown eyes, a small dark nose.
- Cream muzzle and chest.
- Charcoal-coloured paws.
- A big fluffy tail with a light tip.
- A kind, curious and slightly mischievous expression.

Every pose is reviewed to keep these traits; identity takes priority over accidental variations between images.

## Size and space

- Keep the original aspect ratio and `object-fit: contain`.
- Leave room around ears, paws and tail. As a guide, 10 % outer margin.
- For favicons and small avatars, use the [official face icon](favicon.md), with its complete ears and without distorting it. The kit includes variants from 16 to 512 px.
- Check light and dark backgrounds before integrating a pose.
- Keep the transparency and soft edges of the original PNG.

## How he speaks

Toto speaks warmly, in simple sentences. He can greet, accompany a suggestion or celebrate an achievement. Messages avoid jokes at the user's expense and explain actions precisely.

Examples:

- “Hi, I'm Toto. Let's set up your first job.”
- “I have a hint: check the measurements before continuing.”
- “Done! Your work is saved.”

In a product, a celebration pose appears after the operation has really completed. A working pose comes with a textual status; the image alone does not indicate progress or guarantee a result.
