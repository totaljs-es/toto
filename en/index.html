02 / How it all began

Sometimes a community
also needs a smile.

2026

A question.
Big ears.
A name of his own.

“What if our community had a companion?”

The idea was born in Spanish Total.js: we wanted someone to welcome people in our apps, accompany an explanation and celebrate with us when an idea starts to work.

We first thought of a fennec fox. We loved its enormous ears and that look of always being about to discover something. Then a European red fox (Vulpes vulpes) brought us closer to two places in our story: Spain, where our community grows, and Slovakia, where Total.js was born.

We kept the ears. The orange fur, the big tail and a smile followed. And the name: Toto, short, friendly, with a small nod to Total.js.

He loves the moment when someone says:
“That's it. Now it works.”

03 / A real fox

European red fox.
Vulpes vulpes.

Toto is not just any fox: he is a European red fox, the one that lives in the forests, pastures and fields of Spain and Slovakia. He has the traits of his species, so you recognize him at a glance.

Common name

Scientific name

Family

Ears
